Your privacy, at a glance

A comfortable stay starts with peace of mind. Here is how we look after your information, and how you stay in control.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This notice describes how the Vila Sanja website handles personal data. Vila Sanja is operated by Sanja Volner and Matej Abramović, Selo Varoš 70 D, 52100 Premantura, Croatia. For privacy questions or requests, contact vila-sanja@hotmail.com or call +385 95 544 5102.

Optional services rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Website delivery and security rely on the legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Article 6(1)(f)). You can choose maps and reviews separately. Withdrawal stops future loading; it cannot undo information already sent or delete provider cookies. You can delete those through your browser settings. Contact details and booking information are needed to answer an enquiry or arrange a stay; without them we may be unable to do so. We do not make solely automated decisions about you.

Fonts, icons and the Vila Sanja logo are hosted with this website and do not contact external font services.

Data we process and why

This website has no contact or booking form and does not take payments. If you contact the hosts by email, phone, or WhatsApp, the information you provide (such as contact details, requested dates, and messages) is used to answer your enquiry and, if you book, to arrange and fulfil your stay. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract and performing the booking contract; applicable legal obligations may also require keeping booking and accounting records. WhatsApp is an optional third-party communication service; if you choose it, your use is also subject to Meta's privacy terms.

The hosting provider necessarily receives technical connection data such as your IP address, request time, and requested page to deliver and protect the website. These technical logs are retained by the hosting provider for its operational and security purposes. The hosts do not use this site to build visitor profiles, serve targeted advertising. Visitor counts are provided by Flag Counter as described below.

External content and browser storage

Optional external content is disabled unless you allow it in the privacy settings. If allowed, the site can request Google Maps and the Elfsight guest-review widget from their providers. Those providers may receive your IP address and browser/device information and may store or read information on your device under their own policies: Google, Elfsight. You can refuse or withdraw this choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer. Maps can also be opened directly on Google Maps.

The site uses browser local storage (not advertising cookies) to remember your chosen language (vs-lang), display theme (vs-theme), and optional-content preference (vs-privacy-consent-v3). The preference record contains the choices for maps and reviews, a notice version, and the date, expires after 180 days, and is stored on your device. Language and theme are saved until you clear your browser storage. No analytics or advertising cookies are set by the site.

Recipients, transfers, and retention

Enquiry and booking information is accessible to the hosts and the providers needed to communicate or deliver the website (such as email and hosting providers). Optional external services receive data only if you enable them or follow an external link. Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area; their privacy policies describe the applicable transfer safeguards. Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond and manage any resulting booking. Booking, tax, and accounting records are retained for the periods required by Croatian law. The hosting provider determines its technical-log retention period under its service and security policies.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions in data-protection law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time for future processing. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal. To exercise a right, contact the hosts using the details above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), azop.hr, or your local supervisory authority.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the website or its data practices change. The current version and update date will be published on this page.

Flag Counter

The footer displays Flag Counter (counter ID fMiv) to count visits and show visitor countries. Its image loads automatically when the footer approaches the screen, independently of the map and review preferences. This sends a request, including your IP address and browser information, to s01.flagcounter.com.
