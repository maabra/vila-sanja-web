About Vila Sanja

Hosts: Sanja Volner & Matej Abramović

We are a mother and son who take care of the house together.

Vila Sanja is near the village centre, the sea and Cape Kamenjak. The property has a duplex apartment for 6, an apartment for 4 and a room with a bathroom and balcony.

The property is surrounded by greenery. Private parking is available in the courtyard.

The hosts live on site and can answer questions about the property and local area.

Staff speaks: German, English
