About the Village

Premantura is at the southernmost point of Istria, near Pula. The village has shops, restaurants and cafés.

Cape Kamenjak can be reached by car, bicycle or on foot from the village.

Vila Sanja is 300 m from the village centre.

  • Supermarket, bakery & pharmacy in village
  • Restaurants and cafés along the seafront
  • Pula city center ~20 min by car
  • Cape Kamenjak entrance ~5 min by car
  • Rovinj ~45 min, Poreč ~1 hr by car
  • Accommodation for families
