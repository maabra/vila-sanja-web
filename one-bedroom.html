One-Bedroom Apartment

Accommodation photographs

Apartment for up to 4 guests

With a private entrance, this air-conditioned 35 m² apartment features a separate bedroom, living room and bathroom with shower and hairdryer. The fully equipped kitchen includes a stovetop, oven, refrigerator, kettle and kitchenware. A terrace with sea and garden views, flat-screen TV with cable and satellite channels, and free WiFi complete the space.

  • Up to 2 + 2 people
  • Full kitchen with dining area
  • Private balcony
